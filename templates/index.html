About StudyHub

A simple platform built to make study resources easier to access.

StudyHub brings learning material together in one place. Instead of searching different websites for notes, PDFs and lectures, students can access useful study resources through a simple dashboard.

The goal of StudyHub is simple: provide students and beginner programmers with accessible learning material and a clean learning experience.
